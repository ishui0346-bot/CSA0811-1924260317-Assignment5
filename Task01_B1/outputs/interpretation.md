# B1.4 Interpretation of the charts (numbers computed by the program)

1. **Trend (line)** - Adyar's daily mean PM2.5 ranges from 60.6 to 67.0 µg/m³
   over October; 31 of 31 days are above the 60 µg/m³ line. A line chart is used because time
   is ordered and the eye follows the slope and spikes.
2. **Stations (bar)** - Porur is the most polluted station (76.2) and Guindy the cleanest
   (43.8). The bar chart is sorted so the ranking is read at once. The box plot shows that the spread inside a
   station is also large, so means alone hide the bad hours.
3. **Temperature vs PM2.5 (scatter)** - Pearson correlation = -0.09. A weak relationship: this is correlation, not causation -
   both temperature and PM2.5 depend on the hour of the day (traffic and boundary-layer effects).
4. **Worst hours (bar)** - Worst hour is 9:00 (77.0), best is 15:00 (46.9); the morning (8-10) and
   evening (18-21) rush hours form the two peaks, which suggests traffic is a major source.
